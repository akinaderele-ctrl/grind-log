About this plan
52 weeks, Oct 5 2026 to Oct 3 2027. Three gym sessions a week at 30 to 35 minutes, built as two loops of three exercises at a single station. Each season swaps the exercise menu for variety and matches Chicago weather: outdoor walks in fall, spring and summer, indoor finishers in winter. Target pace is 0.5 lb a week, roughly 250 calories a day under maintenance. Machine layouts differ by gym, so if a loop crosses your floor, use the gym swap for that exercise.
