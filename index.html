About this plan
52 weeks, Oct 5 2026 to Oct 3 2027. Three gym sessions a week at 33 minutes (40 with a bonus exercise and a longer finisher), built as two loops of three exercises at a single station, plus an optional cardio-led Workout D in weeks you have room. The goal is all eight key areas at least once a month and 150 minutes of cardio a week. Each season swaps the exercise menu for variety and matches Chicago weather: outdoor walks in fall, spring and summer, indoor finishers in winter. Target pace is 0.5 lb a week, roughly 250 calories a day under maintenance. Machine layouts differ by gym, so if a loop crosses your floor, use the gym swap for that exercise.
